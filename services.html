BEFORE WE MEET

A few things to know.

Clear expectations make for a more relaxed day.

What if plans change?

If you cancel or do not attend, the deposit is non-refundable. Unavoidable circumstances can be handled by rescheduling. If I cancel, your deposit is refunded in full.

What counts toward the session time?

Late arrival and travel between shooting locations count toward the booked time. Extra time is billed in 30-minute blocks, including any part of a block. Please agree additional time before continuing.

Are tickets and transport included?

Entry tickets and travel costs are reimbursed separately. We’ll discuss the locations and related expenses before confirming the session.

When do I pay and receive the photos?

The remaining balance is due immediately after the shoot. We look through the photos together so you can raise any concerns then. All session photos are delivered within 24 hours.

Can I keep my photos private?

Session images may be shared in my portfolio or on social channels. If you prefer not to have yours shared, please let me know before the shoot.

Planning a company event or exhibition?

Send your brief, schedule and coverage needs so we can discuss the assignment and a separate quote.
